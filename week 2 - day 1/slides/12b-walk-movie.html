Answer: B — Dune sci-fi movie in 1984. Step through slowly and do not skip ahead. Line 3 is the first half of the lecture: Movie film2 = film1 copies the reference, not the object, so there is one object with two names — and line 4 writes 1984 into the object film1 is looking at. Line 7 is the second half: film2 is re-pointed at film3’s object, and from there on it has nothing to do with film1, so line 8 never touches it. Note what does not happen here: nothing becomes unreachable, because film1 held on the whole time. Wrong answers to name afterwards — A assumes film2 got a copy of the object; C assumes the last updateYear reaches everything; D assumes the object remembers its history.
