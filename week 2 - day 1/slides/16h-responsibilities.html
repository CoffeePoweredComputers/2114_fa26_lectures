Your responsibilities

A checklist of six: work through the content and the assignments yourself, document every use of an LLM, understand and verify anything it gives you, meet the learning objectives unassisted, be ready to write code from scratch, and be comfortable with thorough tests, the debugger and Python Tutor.

  • Work through the course content and the assignments yourself.
  • Document every use of an LLM.
  • Understand and verify anything it hands you.
  • Meet the learning objectives without assistance.
  • Be ready to write code from scratch, unaided.
  • Be comfortable with thorough tests, the debugger, and Python Tutor.
Read them as a list of capabilities rather than a list of prohibitions — every line is something they will be asked to do in a room with no laptop. The last one is the practical one: the debugger and Python Tutor answer the same questions an LLM would, faster, and about their actual code.
