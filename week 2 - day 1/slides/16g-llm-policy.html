THE RULE

Using an LLM in CS 2114

Two cards side by side. Outside class, using an LLM to help you learn is your choice, and you document that use the way the syllabus and each assignment describe. In class, no: the tests and the final exam are the largest part of the grade and you write them unaided. A line underneath says both halves are the same rule.

outside class

Your choice — and you write it down

You may use an LLM to help you learn. You document that use, exactly the way the syllabus and each assignment ask for it.

in class

No — not on any test

The in-class tests and the final exam are the largest single part of your grade, and you write them with no assistance of any kind.

Both halves are the same rule from two sides: whatever help you take, the understanding has to end up in your head.

Read the documentation requirement as a requirement, not a suggestion — undocumented use is an honour-code matter, documented use is not. Then say the second card plainly and without threat: the exam is simply the day the scaffolding is gone, and it is worth knowing that in Week 2 rather than in November.
