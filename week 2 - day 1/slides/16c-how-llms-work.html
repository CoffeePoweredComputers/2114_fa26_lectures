How does an LLM work?

One sentence, large: an LLM uses large-scale association to predict a sequence of tokens. Under it, three consequences — it does next-token prediction over and over, the predictions come from its training data, and its memory is limited — and then, large again, the conclusion: it does not think and it does not problem-solve.

An LLM uses large-scale association to predict a sequence of tokens.

  • So: it predicts the next token, then the next one, then the next one.
  • Its predictions come from what it was trained on.
  • Its memory is limited.

It does not think, and it does not problem-solve.
