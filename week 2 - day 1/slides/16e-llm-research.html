What the research says about students and LLMs

Three findings, each with its source. Students using LLMs wrote code from scratch less well but read and traced code about as well as before. Students who reached for AI less, or later in the process, earned higher grades — though nobody has shown which way the arrow points. And students with strong foundations gained more from the tools, while students without one were often held back.

All three point the same way: the tool multiplies what you already have. In Week 2 of CS 2114, what you already have is the thing we are building.

Be honest about the second card — it is a correlation, and saying so buys credibility for the other two. The third is the one that matters for this room: a CS 2114 student is by definition still building the foundation, which is exactly the population the studies found was hurt rather than helped.
