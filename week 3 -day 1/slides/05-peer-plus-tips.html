Tips for a good group discussion

  • Take turns talking first
  • Agreeing isn’t the finish line
  • Explain why each wrong answer is wrong
    • Why might someone pick it?
  • Repeat back what your group-mate said
    • Saying it yourself helps it stick
Four tips before groups start discussing. First — take turns being the one who answers first, so it isn’t always the same person. Second — agreeing is not the finish line; if everyone landed on the same answer, keep talking through why. Third — go over every wrong answer, not just why the right one is right, and it’s worth asking why a wrong answer might tempt someone — each one is usually written to match a specific misconception, so naming the temptation is most of the learning. Fourth — even when a group-mate already said it clearly, repeat it back yourself: “so what I think you said was …” feels redundant, but saying it in your own words is what makes it stick.
